About this work

AIR-Fusion adapts a frozen diffusion model for infrared–visible image fusion under degradations such as noise, haze and low light. It aligns infrared information and text instructions with the model, then constrains the denoising process to preserve source-image structure and detail.

Personal projects

SFT training targets and question rewording

Qwen2.5-0.5B / LoRA / Reproducible experiment

A small text-only study with 96 synthetic training examples, two answer targets and two seeds. The follow-up tests alternate question wording and reports final numeric answers separately from output format.

Training code, fixed inputs, raw responses and scoring decisions are public. The reports discuss the unequal supervision budgets and the limits of the results.

paired results/code and data/data review examples

SFT Rewrite Checker

JavaScript / Training data review / Runs in your browser

Compare the complete input, original answer and rewritten answer. Simple rules surface changes in numbers, condition cues, certainty, clarification requests and formatting for human review.

Import JSON or JSONL, filter the review queue, and export decisions with notes. The tool keeps data in the current page and does not call a model or upload records.

Speech Evaluation Demo

Python / SQLite / Speech evaluation

A small personal project for comparing reference transcripts with recognition outputs. It calculates character error rate and shows the insertions, deletions and substitutions behind each score.

Compare two transcript versions on the same references, filter regressions, and inspect character edits. Includes synthetic examples and local CSV import.
